Two things, done well.

Muninn keeps a small, sharp scope: a place to keep reusable context, and a way to tighten the prompt you are about to send.

Context cards you can reuse

Write a block of context once, then inject it into any chat input with a click. It appends to what you have typed, never overwrites it.

  • Create, edit, pin, and fuzzy-search by title or tag.
  • Markdown bodies with a live preview. Pinned cards sort first.
  • An optional template wraps the context so the model reads it first.

Sharpen a prompt before you send it.

Pick a tone (Auto, Business, Creative, Technical) and a revision strength (Conservative, Balanced, Radical). Run it from the popup, or from the dock right on the page.

Offline rules, zero network

A deterministic pipeline adds a persona, requests an output format, and expands terse prompts. It cannot make a network call.

Bring your own key

Your provider, your key

Calls Anthropic or OpenAI with your own key. On-page requests route through the background worker, so your key never enters the web page.

key stays on device

It shows up where you already work

A small pair of buttons sits just above the chat composer: the raven opens your cards, the second opens prompt upgrade. If a site changes its layout, the dock hides itself quietly instead of breaking the page.

  • One consistent look across every supported site.
  • Turn the dock, capture, or prompt upgrade off at any time.
Claude ChatGPT Gemini

Runs only on claude.ai, chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, and gemini.google.com. No broad page access.

Private by design, not by promise.

Muninn is fully client-side. There is nothing to log in to and nowhere for your data to go.

Stays on your device

Cards and settings live in your browser's local storage. Nothing syncs to your Google account or any server.

No analytics, ever

No telemetry, no usage counting, no token meter, no remote configuration. The code makes no background calls.

Your key, your control

A BYOK key is stored locally, masked in the UI, scrubbed from errors, and sent only to the provider you chose.
